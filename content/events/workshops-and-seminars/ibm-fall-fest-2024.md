# Qiskit Fall Fest 2024

Clemson Quantum Club hosted Qiskit Fall Fest 2024, part of the student-run
event series that IBM Quantum supports on campuses around the world every
fall. The day featured hands-on Qiskit workshops, live demos, and networking
with IBM quantum engineers, open to students of all experience levels.
