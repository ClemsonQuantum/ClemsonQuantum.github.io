# IBM Quantum Qiskit Fall Fest 2024

Clemson Quantum Club hosted IBM Quantum Qiskit Fall Fest 2024, part of IBM's
annual global series of student-run quantum computing events. The day featured
hands-on Qiskit workshops, live demos, and networking with IBM quantum
engineers, open to students of all experience levels.
