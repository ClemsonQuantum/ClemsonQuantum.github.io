Clemson sent four teams and 18 students to the first SC Quantathon, a 24-hour quantum hackathon held in Columbia in October 2024, and came home with wins in every category. The grand prize was a trip to the NYUAD International Hackathon for Social Good in Abu Dhabi the following April.
