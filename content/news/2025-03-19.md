Clemson News profiles the Clemson Quantum Club in its first year: biweekly meetings in McAdams Hall, Play-Doh quantum chess, and groups of about 20 students traveling to each hackathon. The club won every category at the first SC Quantathon, including the grand prize, and several students placed at MIT’s iQuHack.
