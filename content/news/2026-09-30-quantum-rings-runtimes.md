Quantum Rings, a challenge sponsor of SC Quantathon v3 at Clemson’s Watt Family Innovation Center (September 25–27, 2026), recaps its challenge: teams analyzed QASM files and built machine learning models that predict how long a circuit will take to run on the Quantum Rings simulator without running it. The Wolfpack (Andrew Cox, Jaber Ibne Taher, Caroline Taylor, Murat Önem, and Robert Maguire) won the challenge.
